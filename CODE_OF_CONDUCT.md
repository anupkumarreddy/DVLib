# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Accept responsibility for
mistakes, focus feedback on ideas and code, and avoid harassment, insults,
discrimination, threats, or publishing another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through their
GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially
as practical. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
